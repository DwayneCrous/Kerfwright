# Project status and caveats

This page holds the phase-by-phase status of Kerfwright, with every measured result and every caveat. The [README](../README.md) carries the short version. Numbers here are measured by the acceptance scripts in `scripts/`; the linked phase reports give the method and the criteria that are only partly met.

## Where the project stands

This repository holds the **research, specification and architecture blueprint** plus a Cargo workspace. **Phase 1 (math foundation, kernel skeleton, tessellator, exporters, wgpu viewer) is implemented** — see [`docs/phase1-report.md`](phase1-report.md) for results against the acceptance criteria and a list of known gaps. **Phase 2 (sketcher & constraint solver, DXF, egui sketcher app) is implemented** — see [`docs/phase2-report.md`](phase2-report.md). **Phase 3 (B-rep modelling kernel: SSI, Booleans, extrude/revolve/sweep/loft, fillet/chamfer, shell/offset/draft, mass properties, STEP, IGES) is implemented** — see [`docs/phase3-report.md`](phase3-report.md) for the measured results, the criteria that are only partly met, and the oracle set-up. **Phase 4 (parametric engine: expressions with units, incremental evaluator with early cutoff, persistent topological names, `.kwf` file format, timeline edits, assemblies, sheet metal, SubD, scripting) is implemented** — see [`docs/phase4-report.md`](phase4-report.md); two of its criteria are met only in part (cold replay of one long linear chain; exact G1 at SubD extraordinary vertices). **Phase 5 (CAM with adaptive clearing, drop-cutter, tri-dexel stock, five-axis kinematics and five post-processors; tetrahedral meshing and TET10 FEA with SIMP; ECAD with placement, routing, DRC, pour and fabrication outputs; OIT/GTAO/path-traced rendering with a denoiser) is implemented** — see [`docs/phase5-report.md`](phase5-report.md); its criteria are met as measured, but the suites, simulators and viewers it uses are defined and written in this repository, the adaptive-clearing freedom-to-operate review is not done, and the IDF bottom-side convention is unverified. Phase 6 is not started. **The application** (`kw-app`: welcome page, projects, and the Sketch / Model / CAM / FEA / ECAD / Render workspaces) is described in [`docs/app-shell.md`](app-shell.md); the Model workspace exposes only a few of the engine's features so far.

## Results and caveats

* Phase 3 meets its six acceptance criteria as measured (details and caveats in [`docs/phase3-report.md`](phase3-report.md)): Boolean fuzz 100,000 pairs (99.90 % on valid-expected cases, 0 invalid outputs, 0 wrong results found by an independent point-classification check), free-form-body fuzz 100,000 pairs (99.61 %, 0 invalid, 0 identity violations), SSI (including spline/spline), fillet / chamfer benchmark 500/500 with tangent chains, vertex blends, variable-radius fillets, STEP round trip with OpenCASCADE, truck and Gmsh, 259/259 public-corpus files, and the Boolean (500 ms) and fillet (300 ms) SLAs. A Boolean *heal* stage retries failed or doubtful operations and only returns verified results. Not done: vertex blends beyond three equal-radius planar faces (other corners meet in a mitre by design), variable radius on ellipse / spline edges and on sphere / torus / spline faces, setback, and one fuzz seed (34267) where the point oracle and a free-form Boolean result disagree and the cause is not found.
* Phase 2 meets all six automated criteria; the drag-latency SLA is asserted on decomposable sketches and is exceeded on row-coupled ones (8.6 ms p99 at 500 entities), G3/mirror/pattern are not done, and the sketcher window has been smoke-tested but not visually reviewed — details in the Phase 2 report.
* Phase 1 is complete against its automated criteria; the exceptions (published Piegl–Tiller examples beyond Ex. 2.3, opening exports in third-party viewers, "reference GPU" undefined) are listed in the Phase 1 report.
* Phase 4 results (details and caveats in [`docs/phase4-report.md`](phase4-report.md)): naming corpus 1 716 scenarios, 0 silent wrong resolutions, 99.9 % correct; identical geometry and names on 1 / 4 / 32 threads; no-op edits re-execute nothing; cold replay of a 200-feature model of eight parts 0.34 s with a 53 ms tail edit, but **8.7 s / 0.2 s for one linear chain of 100 Booleans**; timeline refusals judged like an independent oracle in 100 % of 2 800+ cases; sheet-metal flat lengths exact to 1e-14 mm; SubD within 0.01 mm of the limit surface with 0.4–1.4° worst tangent mismatch at extraordinary vertices. The timeline UI was headless (engine + API) when Phase 4 was done; the application now has one (see [`app-shell.md`](app-shell.md)).
* Phase 5 results (details and caveats in [`docs/phase5-report.md`](phase5-report.md)): adaptive clearing 1.010 × target engagement on 50 seeded pockets; 0 gouges and 0 holder collisions on 21 parts; tri-dexel volumes within 0.13 %; FK(IK) 2.6e-12 on 5 machines; five dialects replay 50 five-axis programs; TET10 benchmarks within 0.1–1.8 %; 100 k DOF in 3.6 s and 1 M DOF PCG in 81 s; SIMP MBB 216.81; 1 023/1 023 nets routed with 0 DRC violations on 20 boards; STEP placement error 8e-8 mm; 1 M triangles at 2.6 ms p95 (1080p); path tracer 0.8 % relative MSE at 16 spp, denoised 30.5 dB at 64 spp.
* Surfaces (the SURFACE tab, surface analysis, continuity): results, sources and limits in [`docs/surface-report.md`](surface-report.md); `scripts/surface_acceptance.sh` runs its checks and takes the screenshots.
* Statements about proprietary kernels (ASM, Parasolid, ACIS) are labelled **[Public]** or **[Inferred]** in the research docs; nothing here is derived from Autodesk code or binaries.
* Performance numbers in the PRD are targets; measured Phase 1 numbers are in the report.
